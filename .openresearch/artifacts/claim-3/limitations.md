# Claim 3 limitations and deviations

- This verifies the definitional and strict-extension content of Claim 3. It
  does not by itself verify Theorem 3.4.
- The witness loss is intentionally minimal and independent of the test point.
  That is allowed by Assumption 3.1 but is not presented as a useful predictor.
- Exact algebra, rather than a finite Monte Carlo sample, is the main evidence.
- The verifier is a purpose-built certificate checker, not a proof-assistant
  kernel. The derivation is short enough to audit line by line and is checked
  again by an independent implementation.
