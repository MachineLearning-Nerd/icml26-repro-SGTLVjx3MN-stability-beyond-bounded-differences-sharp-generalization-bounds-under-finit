# Claim 2 four-route method

1. **Displayed-proof audit.** Reconstruct each step and test equation (61).
   The committed Bernoulli example rejects that step.
2. **Complete declared finite grids.** Enumerate every function on binary
   product spaces with the declared output grids, construct minimal valid
   coordinate envelopes, and test tail and envelope discontinuities.
3. **Independent fallback derivation.** Von Bahr–Esseen/Markov yields a
   universal polynomial finite-`p` bound, but not the exact `Q`-dependent form.
4. **Mandatory falsification route.** Deterministically search 20,000
   assumption-satisfying discrete product problems across `n`, skewed product
   probabilities, heavy output scales, `p`, `Q`, and `z`.

No failed proof step or absence of a counterexample is mislabeled as
falsification. If route 4 finds no valid contradiction and route 1 remains
unrepaired, the honest verdict is `BLOCKED`.
