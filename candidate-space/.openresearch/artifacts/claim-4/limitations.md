# Claim 4 limitations and deviations

No full-scale dataset is needed because the paper claim is a universal
mathematical implication, not an empirical performance number. The main
evidence is therefore a symbolic derivation. The executable certificate is
independently checked but is not encoded in a proof-assistant kernel.

The finite Bernoulli enumeration is deliberately scoped as a diagnostic and
does not establish the theorem's universal quantifiers. The explicit
constants are valid consequences of Theorem 2.2; they are not claimed to be
optimal.
