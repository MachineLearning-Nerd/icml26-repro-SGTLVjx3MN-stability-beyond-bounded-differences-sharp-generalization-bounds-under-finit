# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

- The universal result is supported by an independently reconstructed
  derivation, not inferred from the exhaustive finite diagnostic.
- The custom checker is not a general-purpose proof-assistant kernel. Its
  constant algebra and finite enumeration are independently implemented, while
  the short universal derivation remains human-auditable.
- This verifies the inequality and two-regime form. It does not independently
  prove the paper's broader informal discussion of minimax optimality; the paper
  itself defines “sharp” only in tail-order terms.
